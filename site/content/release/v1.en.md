**Draft** — This page is not published: the founder reviews it first. Krizaka Agents is our own private engineering system, used internally every day; there is nothing to buy or to request here, and no date is promised.

The companion page lists every [renamed environment variable](/migration/).

### § 1 — Who it serves and what it reaches

Krizaka Agents sits around coding sessions so that an issue becomes a checked, merged pull request. Every diagram on this page is laid out from the system's own architecture model, never drawn by hand: when the model changes, the drawing changes with it.

<!--release-view:v1_c1-->

### § 2 — The core talks to ports

The parts that decide (the session builder, the commands, the trains, the coordinator, the watchdog) depend on interfaces, not on a vendor. A missing or unknown setting stops the start instead of surfacing later.

<!--release-view:v1_c2-->

Each interface has one adapter, and the adapter is where a vendor's name lives.

<!--release-view:v1_adapters-->

### § 3 — The tower

What watches the work: the automatic launchers, the watchdog, the credential rotation, the operations service and the local cockpit. A model never decides when an alert closes.

<!--release-view:v1_tower-->

### § 4 — One event format, one sink

The session builder, the commands and the trains write one event format, to one place, and it leaves as OpenTelemetry logs.

### § 6 — The environment, renamed

Settings are named by the role they serve, not by the organisation or the vendor behind them. For one release, an old name still resolves through the alias table with a warning that names the new one; after that, the old name is refused.

The [migration page](/migration/) lists every old name and its new one.
